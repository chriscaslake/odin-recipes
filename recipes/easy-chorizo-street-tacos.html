Easy Chorizo Street Tacos

Description

  • Features chorizo cooked with choptle peppers for a bold smoky taste.
  • Chorizo grease creates layers of flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 Chorizo sausage link, casing removed and meat crumbled
  • 2 Tablespoons chipotle peppers in adobo sauce
  • 4 corn tortillas
  • 2 tablespoons chopped onion, or to taste
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro, or to taste (optional)

Directions

  1. Combine crumbed chorizo and chipotle peppers in adobo sauce in a bowl.
  2. Heat skillet over medium-high heat; add chorizo mixture and cook until crisp, 5 to 7 minutes. Transfer to a place, reserving grease in skillet
  3. Heat tortillas in reserved grease in skillet over medium heat until warmed, 1 to 2 minutes per side. Stack 2 tortillas for each taco, then fill with chorizo, onion, and cilantro.
