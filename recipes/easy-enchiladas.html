Easy Enchiladas

Description

Creay, satisfying beef-and-cheese filling.

Super simple enchildas that require just five ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 2 (16 ounce) jars of prepared salsa
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 (15.5 ounce) jar prepared salsa con queso
  • 20 (8 inch) flour tortillas
  • 1 (8 ounce) package shredded Cheddar-Monterey Jack cheese blend.
