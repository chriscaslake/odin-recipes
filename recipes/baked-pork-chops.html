Baked Pork Chops with Cream of Mushroom Soup

Description

  • Baked with cream of mushroom soup until fork-tender
  • A go-to for busy nights

Ingredients

  • 1/2 yellow onion, slices
  • 4 boneless pork chops
  • 26 ounces condense cream of mushroom soup
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 2 cubes beef bouillon

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 325 degrees F (165 degrees C)
  2. Place onion slices on the bottom fo a glass baking dish. Lay porkchops over onions. Cover chops evenly with condensed soup.
  3. Add water and crumble the bouillon cubes evenly over the top. Cover the baking dish with aluminum foil.
  4. Bake porkchops in the preheated over, stirring gravy once or twice, until no longer pink in the center, about 1 hour. An instant-read thermometer inserted into the center of a chop should read at least 145 degrees F (63 degrees C)
